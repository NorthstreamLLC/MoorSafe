The product

The MoorSafe 400 lb mooring anchor.

A mushroom anchor with three bars that form a cone above the stem. The chain has nothing to catch on, which significantly reduces the risk of chain wrap. Designed, built and tested in Maine.

How it's built

A mushroom anchor with one flaw removed.

A cone, not a post

Three symmetrical bars rise from the bowl to a single point above the stem. The chain has nothing to catch on, so it slides off as the boat swings.

The same proven bowl

The same concave mushroom bowl. It still beds into mud and gains suction over weeks, so holding power isn't compromised.

Cast iron and welded steel

A cast-iron base with welded steel bars, finished in high-visibility safety orange. In tensile testing, a 300 lb prototype held to 40,657 lb.

Fit

Is the 400 lb right for your mooring?

The 400 lb is recommended for boats of about 30 to 40 ft, or for heavier displacement and moderate exposure. Sizing depends on displacement, scope and exposure, so we confirm it with you before you order.
