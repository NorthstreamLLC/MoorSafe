Description

  • Mushroom dish that beds into mud and gains suction over time, like the anchor it replaces
  • Three symmetrical bars meet above the stem, leaving nowhere for the chain to catch
  • Large top eye for a proper mooring shackle
  • Same chain, shackles, swivel, pennant and scope as a standard mushroom mooring
  • Used on moorings for a range of boats. The right anchor depends on your boat, bottom and exposure, so we size it with you
  • High-visibility safety orange, easy to spot when the mooring is hauled

Installation

  1. Size itTell us about your boat and mooring. We confirm the anchor before you order.
  2. Rig itSame chain, shackles, swivel and pennant you use today. Attach at the top eye.
  3. Set itLower with a mooring barge or service boat, at the same scope and practice as a mushroom.
  4. Let it bedHolding grows as the dish embeds. Inspect on your normal schedule.
