Frequently asked

Before you reach out.

What's in the info packet?

Anchor specs and weights, pricing once available, installation guidance, the full Yale Cordage and Casco Bay test results, and a sizing recommendation for your specific harbor and boat.

How quickly do you respond?

Within one business day, in most cases. We're a small Maine team — your message goes to a real person, not a queue.

Are pre-orders open yet?

Pre-orders open later this season. If you fill out the form, we'll add you to the early list and send pricing the moment it's set.

I'm a harbormaster — can MoorSafe present at our association meeting?

Yes. Scott has presented to the Maine Harbor Master's Association and is happy to talk with other regional groups. Mention the meeting in your message and we'll coordinate.

Do you ship outside Maine / outside the U.S.?

Yes — we can ship anywhere in the continental U.S. International shipping case-by-case. Tell us where you are and we'll work it out.
