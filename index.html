Common questions

What harbormasters and boat owners ask first.

Does the MoorSafe anchor really stop chain wrap?

The three bars form a cone above the stem, so the chain has nothing to catch on and slides off as the boat swings. In the Casco Bay field test, the diver found no chain on the MoorSafe stem after 28 days of tide.

Is it as strong as a regular mushroom anchor?

Stronger in the test. At Yale Cordage, a 300 lb MoorSafe prototype held to 40,657 lb, against 38,560 lb for a 300 lb traditional mushroom. That is about 5% higher.

Do I need different chain or hardware?

No. Use the same chain, shackles, swivel and pennant you use today, attached at the top eye. Set it with a mooring barge or service boat, at the same scope you would use for a mushroom.

Is the 400 lb right for my boat?

The 400 lb is recommended for boats of about 30 to 40 ft, or for heavier displacement and moderate exposure. We confirm the size with you before you order.
