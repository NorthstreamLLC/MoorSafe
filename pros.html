For harbormasters & mooring services

Take chain wrap out of your mooring field.

Wrapped chain is the problem you hear about at high tide. MoorSafe removes the catch point on the anchor itself, and it installs exactly like the mushroom you already set.

Why it fits the way you already work.

Same gear, same practice

Same chain, shackles, swivel and pennant. Attach at the top eye and set it with a mooring barge or service boat at the scope you already use.

Far less to check for

Three bars form a cone above the stem, so the chain slides off. Inspect on your normal schedule. In the Casco Bay test, 28 days of tide wrapped nothing.

Tested outside our own bench

Tensile testing at Yale Cordage in Saco, and a 28-day field test on the seabed in Casco Bay, each head-to-head against a traditional mushroom.

Made in Maine

Designed, manufactured and tested in Maine by people whose boats depend on the result. Presented to the Maine Harbor Masters Association.
