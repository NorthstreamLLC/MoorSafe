Field test · Aug 2026 · 3 min read

28 days on the bottom of Casco Bay

Two anchors, one muddy seabed, a lobster boat, and a diver. Why MoorSafe came up fully buried — and why that matters more than the breakout number.

We set both anchors on the same soft bottom and left them alone for 28 days. Then a diver went down before the pull. The traditional mushroom was partially buried with the stem exposed. The MoorSafe anchor was fully buried.

Breakout at 3:1 scope, read on a 4,000 lb digital indicator: 1,575 lb for the traditional anchor, 1,295 lb for MoorSafe. Comparable — and the deeper anchor keeps gaining as sediment consolidates above it. A mushroom that has stopped sinking has stopped improving.

The other thing the diver noted: no chain on the MoorSafe stem. Twenty-eight days of tide had wrapped nothing.
