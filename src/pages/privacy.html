Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

MoorSafe ("we", "us") is based in Maine, USA. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email us at {{email}}.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you request a quote, ask a question, request the info packet, or contact us, we collect what you type into the form: your name, email address, phone number (if you give one), your role (for example boat owner, harbormaster or mooring service), your organization or harbor, your boat length, and your message. If you tick the box to receive updates, we record that too.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider and any analytics tools we use may collect basic technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, and the site you came from. Analytics tools may use cookies or similar technology.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your requests, send you the info packet or a quote, follow up on your inquiry, improve the website, keep it secure, and meet legal obligations. We send occasional MoorSafe updates only if you opt in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the website and our business, such as website hosting, form and spreadsheet tools, email, and analytics. These providers may process your information on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as we need it to respond to you and to keep reasonable business records, then delete or anonymize it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing {{email}}. You can unsubscribe from updates using the link in any update email, or by emailing us. You can limit cookies through your browser settings.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no website or online service can be completely secure.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
