01 · The problem

Every mushroom anchor in a soft-bottom harbor has the same flaw.

The cast-iron mushroom anchor sinks into mud and gains suction over time — but its vertical stem is its weakness. As tides, currents, and wind swing a moored boat through 360°, the chain rotates around the stem. Once it catches, every cycle pulls it tighter. Effective scope shrinks, peak loads spike, mooring balls pull under at high tide, and chain or shackle failure becomes a question of when, not if.

Traditional mushroom — the stem becomes a winch.
The MoorSafe fix

Three bars. Conical shape. No wrap.

Three symmetrically positioned bars rise from the bowl to a single point above the stem, forming a cone. The chain has nothing to catch on. The bowl still beds in, still gains suction, still holds — and tensile strength is higher than the original.

04 · Installation

Installs exactly like the anchor it replaces.

1
Size it

Match weight to boat length, displacement, scope, and exposure — or ask us.

2
Rig it

Same chain, shackles, swivel, and pennant you use today. Attach at the top eye.

3
Set it

Lower with a mooring barge or service boat, same scope and practice as a mushroom.

4
Let it bed

Holding grows as the bowl embeds. Inspect on your normal schedule — no wrap to check for.

Any mooring service provider will be familiar with the process. We can recommend providers in your area.
